About RoadGuard

Turning everyday commutes into safer roads

Potholes like this one form quietly — a small crack widens with every rainfall and every truck that passes, until it becomes a hazard for cars, bikes, and pedestrians alike. RoadGuard gives anyone the power to report it the moment they see it.

Snap a photo, drop a pin, and your report joins a shared map that local road teams and fellow drivers can see. No login is needed to browse what's already been reported — just sign up when you're ready to submit your own.

Know the Hazard

What does a pothole actually look like?

A pothole starts as a small crack in the road surface. Once water seeps in and freezes, or heavy vehicles repeatedly pound the weak spot, the surface layer breaks away — leaving a bowl-shaped dip with jagged, broken edges like the one pictured here.

Edges like these can catch a tire, bend a rim, or throw a rider off balance, especially when the hole is partly hidden by rainwater. The sooner a pothole is reported with a clear photo and location, the sooner road crews can prioritize and patch it before it grows.

  • Look for broken or crumbling edges around the rim
  • Standing water often hides the true depth
  • Photograph from a safe distance before reporting
